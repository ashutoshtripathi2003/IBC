# Product

> This document records the founder's product vision as given. It must not be extended with
> invented requirements; new requirements are added only when the founder specifies them.

## Identity

**IBC — Indian Business Circle.** A global Professional + Business + Commerce Network.
(Internal codename PROJECT VIJAY is never used publicly.)

IBC brings together professional networking, business networking, company/product/
supplier/buyer discovery, services, communication, RFQs, quotes, negotiation, orders,
payments, reviews, reputation, verification, and AI-powered discovery and matching.

IBC should not copy LinkedIn or IndiaMART; it develops its own identity, business graph,
trust layer, reputation system, search, AI and transaction ecosystem.

## Business graph

Person ↔ Person · Person ↔ Company · Person ↔ Product · Person ↔ Service ·
Company ↔ Company · Company ↔ Product · Company ↔ Service · Company ↔ Supplier ·
Company ↔ Buyer · Person ↔ Opportunity

## Transaction lifecycle

DISCOVER → CONNECT → VERIFY → CHAT → RFQ → QUOTE → COMPARE → NEGOTIATE → ORDER → PAY →
DELIVERY → REVIEW → REPUTATION

## Market strategy

Build for global scale. Launch for local density.
Maharashtra → India → international markets → global. Multi-country, multi-language,
multi-currency, multi-timezone from the start; English first.
